ARCHIVE MATERIAL
================

Scans, photographs of models, screenshots, notebook pages, references.
Name each file after the item's id in src/content/archive.ts, or after what
it is. Both work.

  archive/
    a-001-first-plan.jpg
    a-002-study-model.jpg

A phone photograph of a sketchbook page is fine. Straighten it, crop it to
the page, and it will look at home here.
